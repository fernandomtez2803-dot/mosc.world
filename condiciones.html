1. What MOSC Fabrica is

MOSC Fabrica is an in-house tool of the MOSC project (@mosc.world). It uploads videos made by the MOSC team to the project's own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Who may use it

Only the MOSC team, with the accounts it owns. Using the tool means authorizing it from those accounts through TikTok's and Instagram's own sign-in screens.

3. Content

All published content is created by the MOSC team, which is responsible for it and for complying with TikTok's and Instagram's terms and community guidelines. Scientific facts are checked against their published sources before posting.

4. Platforms

The tool uses the official TikTok and Instagram APIs and follows their terms. TikTok and Instagram are trademarks of their respective owners; MOSC is not affiliated with them.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Instagram app settings. See the privacy policy.

6. Changes and contact

These terms may change; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: direct message to @mosc.world on Instagram or TikTok.
