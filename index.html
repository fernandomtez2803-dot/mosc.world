MOSC Fabrica

MOSC Fabrica is the in-house publishing tool of the MOSC project (@mosc.world on TikTok and Instagram). We make short videos about flies and about how the fruit fly brain was mapped. The tool uploads those videos, made by our own team, to our own accounts.

It is not a public service: nobody else signs in, and it does not read or collect other people's data.
